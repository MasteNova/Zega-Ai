About Arba Minch

Welcome to Arba Minch — Ethiopia's Tropical Rift Oasis

Named after the Amharic words for "Forty Springs", Arba Minch sits nestled between the Guge Mountains and two contrasting Rift Valley lakes: the iron-rich reddish waters of Lake Abaya and the deep blue expanse of Lake Chamo.

Whether you are cruising past giant Nile crocodiles, trekking with zebras in Nechisar National Park, or sharing freshly baked kotcho and handwoven cotton stories with the Dorze people, Arba Minch blends wild nature with warm southern Ethiopian hospitality.

Our Services

Curated Arba Minch Experiences

Everything you need for an unforgettable stay in southern Ethiopia — from boat captains to highland cultural hosts.

Lake Chamo Boat Safaris

Private and small-group motorboat excursions to the famous Crocodile Market, hippo pods, and pelican islands.

Nechisar 4x4 Game Drives

Guided morning and sunset drives across the White Grass plains to spot zebras, Swayne's hartebeest, and greater kudu.

Dorze Highland Tours

Ascend to 2,900m in the Guge Mountains to tour woven bamboo huts, traditional looms, and enset cooking.

Forty Springs Nature Walks

Spot black-and-white colobus monkeys, hornbills, and lush groundwater forest trails right beside town.

Rift-View Lodge Stays

Handpicked stays overlooking the Bridge of God, with sunset terraces, local coffee ceremonies, and fresh fruit.

Omo Valley & Airport Transfers

Seamless pickups from Arba Minch Airport (AMH) and custom onward journeys to Konso, Jinka, and Turmi.
